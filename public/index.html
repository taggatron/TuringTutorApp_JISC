Academic Integrity

Using AI Responsibly

Turing Tutor is designed to support your learning, not do the work for you. Always ensure your submissions represent your own understanding.

JCQ Guidelines Reminder

  • Do not copy and paste AI-generated text directly into your assessments.
  • Use AI for brainstorming, structuring ideas, or improving grammar—not for creating final content.
  • Always acknowledge when and how you have used AI in your work, in accordance with your center's policy.
  • Verify information provided by AI; you are ultimately responsible for the accuracy of your submission.
